PRIVACY

Local-first by design.

Data stored on your Mac

Service discovery, commands, paths, logs, configuration and audit history remain on your Mac. PortDeck does not require an account and does not sell data or use advertising identifiers.

Diagnostics and crashes

Crash diagnostics are off by default and require an explicit opt-in. Diagnostic and configuration files are exported only when you press an export button. They are never uploaded automatically.

Update checks

PortDeck may request public GitHub release metadata to check for a newer version. No local service data is included.

Contact

Use the support channel listed on the PortDeck support page.
